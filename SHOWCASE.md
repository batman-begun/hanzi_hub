# SHÙ 数字 — showcase kit

## LinkedIn

I can recognize Chinese numbers on a page. Catching a price spoken out loud? Different story. 😅

So I built SHÙ 数字 — Mandarin Number Gym.

A small listening workout for four things you actually need: numbers, prices, times, and phone numbers. Listen, type what you heard, check the characters and pinyin, then take another shot at the ones you missed.

144 prepared audio questions. Three levels. Ten questions per workout. No account, no ads.

My favorite decision: no AI call during practice. Mandarin speech is generated ahead of time with an open model; number generation and grading use ordinary code. Less waiting, no per-practice inference bill.

Built as a focused weekend MVP, with mobile browser checks and edge-case tests across 100,000 numbers. The audio is synthetic; native-speaker review is still on my list.

## Batcave project card

**SHÙ 数字 — Mandarin Number Gym**

Know the words. Catch the numbers.

A mobile-first Mandarin listening gym for prices, times, numbers, and phone strings. Ten-question workouts, instant feedback, slow replay, and focused review of missed questions. Built with plain JavaScript and prepared synthetic audio: no signup and no runtime AI calls.

**Role:** Product direction, design, implementation, testing, and shipping with an AI coding collaborator.

**Scope:** 144 prepared clips · 4 workouts · 3 levels · local progress.

**Validation:** Eight automated test groups, numeric roundtrips across 0–99,999, and Chrome desktop/mobile viewport checks. Synthetic pronunciation has not yet been teacher-reviewed.
